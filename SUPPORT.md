# Detour support

**Report a problem or ask a question:** [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/josh345rtfvqw/detour/issues).

It helps to include:
- your browser and its version
- your Detour version, shown at the bottom of the Detour popup
- the YouTube video or channel link where it went wrong

## Common questions

### The "Watch on Nebula/Floatplane" button doesn't appear

- **Check the creator.** Only creators who are also on Nebula or Floatplane get a button.
- **Reload the tab.** If you've just installed or updated Detour, reload the YouTube tab. YouTube keeps old scripts running in tabs that were already open.
- **Check your settings.** Open the Detour popup. If you don't subscribe to that platform, make sure **Show other platforms too** is on.

### It links to the creator's channel instead of the video

Detour couldn't confidently match the video's title, so it links to the channel instead. It never redirects on a guess. Titles sometimes differ between YouTube and the other platform.

### I only see a few Floatplane creators

Log in at [floatplane.com](https://www.floatplane.com), then reopen the Detour popup. Without a login, Detour shows the creators in its own list, which is updated hourly.

### It redirected me when I didn't want it to

- **Stay this time:** click **Stay on YouTube** during the countdown.
- **Stop redirecting:** in the popup, choose **Show a button** instead of **Redirect me**.
