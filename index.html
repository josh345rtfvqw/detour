YouTube videos on
Nebula and Floatplane

Detour checks whether the YouTube video or channel you’re on is also on Nebula or Floatplane, and links to it there. If you subscribe, it can open it there for you.

On a video

A button next to the title

If the video is also on Nebula or Floatplane, a “Watch on Nebula” or “Watch on Floatplane” button appears beside the title, in light or dark to match YouTube.

If you subscribe

Automatic redirects

Tick the services you subscribe to and Detour can open the video there instead. There’s a 3-second countdown with a button to stay on YouTube, and pressing Back returns you to YouTube without redirecting again.

On a channel

Channel pages

A banner under the Subscribe button links to the creator’s Nebula or Floatplane channel. If you dismiss it, it stays hidden for that channel.

Settings

Redirect or button

Choose between redirects and a button, and which service to use when a video is on both. Detour only redirects when it has found the same video. Otherwise it links to the creator’s channel.

Privacy

Detour sends requests to Nebula and Floatplane only for creators who are on those services, and downloads its list of known videos from this site. It doesn’t collect usage data, and your Floatplane login stays with floatplane.com.
