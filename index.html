On a video

A button right next to the title

When a video is also on Nebula or Floatplane, a “Watch on Nebula” or “Watch on Floatplane” button appears beside the title. It uses the platform’s colours and matches YouTube’s light or dark theme.

If you subscribe

Or go there automatically

Tick the subscriptions you have, and Detour can take you straight to the same video. A 3-second countdown lets you stay on YouTube instead, and pressing Back never sends you round in circles.

On a channel

Find creators’ channels too

A banner under the Subscribe button tells you the creator is also on Nebula or Floatplane, with a link to their channel there. Dismiss it once and it stays hidden for that channel.

Your settings

You decide how it works

Choose redirects or just a button. Pick which platform wins when a video is on both. Detour never redirects on a guess: if it can’t find the exact video, it links to the channel instead.

Private by design

Detour has no server and collects no analytics. It talks only to Nebula and Floatplane, directly from your browser, and only for creators who are on those platforms. Your Floatplane login never leaves floatplane.com.
